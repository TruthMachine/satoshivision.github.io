About Satoshi Vision

Satoshi Vision is an independent software development initiative focused on Bitcoin SV. Our work encompasses wallet technology, transaction security, offline signing and tools designed to support the Bitcoin ecosystem. We believe in the importance of individual control, security and the original principles of peer-to-peer electronic cash.
